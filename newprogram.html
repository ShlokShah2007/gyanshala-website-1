B

Brookings Institution — QEI DIB Impact Study

2018–2022 Development Impact Bond 200,000 Students
  • Quality Education India Development Impact Bond (QEI DIB), the largest education impact bond, included Gyan Shala alongside three other providers, focusing on improving literacy and numeracy for 200,000 students across four states in India.
  • Gyan Shala operated community learning centers in underprivileged urban areas and provided targeted teacher support in pedagogy and learning plans.
  • During school shutdowns, Gyan Shala rapidly adapted to phone-based instruction, student worksheets, and small-group TV lessons in community classrooms, continuously iterating strategy based on real-time results.
  • The program produced rare high-quality longitudinal data now used by the Back-to-School Outcomes Fund and other education programs to improve learning outcomes at scale.

D

DFID UK — Model Viability & Replicability Assessment

2007–2008 Bihar Expansion Policy Validated
  • The UK Department for International Development (DFID) conducted a comprehensive analysis of the Gyan Shala model to evaluate its viability, replicability, and long-term sustainability across diverse geographies.
  • DFID validated the model's transportability by supporting Gyan Shala's geographic expansion into Bihar (2007–08), demonstrating it could operate effectively outside its Ahmedabad origins.
  • The study confirmed that Gyan Shala's cost-effectiveness (INR 3,000–6,000 per child annually) combined with measurable outcomes represents a scalable solution to urban education inequality in India.

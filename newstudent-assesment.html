About the Program

External Assessments

Gyan Shala's team of 1,200+ people — teachers, field supervisors, and curriculum designers — implements best-in-class classroom practices including whole-class teaching, peer learning groups, and daily workbooks. Periodic external audits by globally respected agencies validate our impact.

Assessment Framework

Independent Assessment Bodies

Three internationally respected agencies audit Gyan Shala's processes and student outcomes.

EI-India (Educational Initiatives)

Administers the ASSET assessment for Gyan Shala students in Grades 3, 5 and 7 for 2009–18. ASSET is taken by 15,000+ students from 500 schools annually, making it a robust national benchmark against leading CBSE schools.

CfBT Trust – UK

Conducted comprehensive program reviews in 2010 and 2013 covering student attainment, teaching quality, curriculum design, staffing, parent partnerships, and leadership. Benchmarked against UK public schools and international schools in Dubai.

DFID – UK

The UK Department for International Development analysed the Gyan Shala model for viability, replicability, and sustainability. DFID also supported expansion into Bihar (2007–08), validating the model's transportability.
